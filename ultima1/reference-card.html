Surviving the first hour

  1. Ztats. New characters start with Hands and Skin.
  2. In town, Transact at the weapon and armour shops.
  3. Ready the new weapon and armour.
  4. Keep food up: grocers sell it in packs of 10.
  5. At a castle, Transact with the king and offer pence for hit points.
  6. Fight near home until you're stronger; read the signposts.
  7. Q to save outdoors, then back up in the save manager.

The quest

Destroy Mondain's evil gem. The four lands are those of Lord British, of the Feudal Lords, of the Dark Unknown and of Danger and Despair. Kings set quests — slay a monster or visit a landmark — and reward them; four gems (red, green, blue, white) matter in the end. Rescuing a princess grants hit points, pence and experience.
