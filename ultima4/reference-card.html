Thy first hours

  1. Z then 0: see what you own.
  2. Talk to everyone in town, and keep a journal. The clues are in the talk.
  3. Ask people to Join. You'll want all seven companions in the end.
  4. Buy gear, then Ready and Wear it for each player.
  5. Buy food before you leave. Carry torches below ground.
  6. Mix spells ahead of time. You can't cast without a mixture.
  7. Q to save, then back up in the save manager.

Talking

Name Job Health Look Join Give Bye

Then ask about any word that catches your eye in their answers. Four letters is enough. Someone may know more once another person has told you what to ask, so go back.

People may ask you a question. Answer honestly: dishonesty is remembered.

Walking the Path

Honesty · Compassion · Valor · Justice · Sacrifice · Honor · Spirituality · Humility

  • Show generosity to the less fortunate: say Give.
  • Before you fight, ask: is this foe truly evil? If not, stand your ground and let it retreat.
  • Stray from the Path and your companions will desert you.
  • The Seer Hawkwind, in Lord British's castle, reads your progress. Heed him.
  • Each shrine answers a different mantra. Learn them from the people.

Combat

  • North, east, south and west only. Each player acts in turn.
  • Hand weapons reach the next square, polearms reach over a companion, missiles cross the field.
  • Walk a player off the field to save them. Everyone off and the fight ends.
  • Hold a narrow pass: terrain is your friend.
  • Energy fields can only be cast next to the caster.

The twin moons

Moongates appear and vanish with the moons. Trammel tells which gate is active. Once you enter, Felucca tells where you'll come out.

Each moon's cycle: New → Waxing Crescent → First Quarter → Waxing Gibbous → Full → Waning Gibbous → Last Quarter → Waning Crescent. They show at the top of the view.

Finding your way

A sextant reads each coordinate as two letters, latitude then longitude, and the map tool shows the same reading for any square. Horses speed travel on land. Ships fight with broadsides.

The quest

The Triad of Evil is long destroyed. Lord British seeks one who can become the Avatar, a shining example of the Eight Virtues. Many have grown enlightened in one or more, but none have reached the whole. The secrets lie in the hearts of the people and in the land.
